Scope

Changes

  active perks tab. Pressing the gift opens its lid; tab navigation stays native.
- Render earned medals with actual geometry, bevelled edges, metal lighting,
  and a press-to-spin interaction. Preserve earned level colours and labels.
- Bundle both scenes in a transparent offline WebView. No remote JavaScript,
  images, or CDN fetches are needed. Navigation and messages are constrained.
- Unmount offscreen/background scenes, respect reduced motion and guide
  overlays, and retain static artwork if WebGL cannot render.
- Blend video and poster edges into their card surfaces. Request choices and
  selected-problem artwork fill the card edge rather than an inset thumbnail.
  Helper/perks videos and contained scenes also fade into their parent surface.
- Reserve room for text at narrow widths and improve the request heading's
  contrast in dark mode. No play/pause controls were added.
