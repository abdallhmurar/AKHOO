Use case: ui-mockup
Create one highly polished compact mobile-app rewards card concept for AKHOO, an Arabic community roadside assistance app. This is a new visual proposal, called "مفتاح الفزعة", using a beautiful tactile 3D car key as the central metaphor for helping. Not a garage, no people, no medals, no trees, no buildings, no map, no trophies or progress gauge. Show a credible premium app UI, not a promotional poster. Landscape canvas approximately 4:3 with ample margins, one pearl-white rounded rectangular card on very pale ice-blue, all card text flat and crisp in correct RTL Arabic.

Left half: a striking large floating premium car-key fob at a slight three-quarter diagonal, cobalt-blue satin body, deep teal inlay, fine brushed-chrome edges, beautifully rounded geometry, one tasteful small embossed white Arabic "أخوو" brand, two flush subtle unlabelled buttons. A curved silver split key-ring and one short woven teal fabric loop make it unmistakably a car key. Single small warm-gold metal end accent reflects current gold rank; NOT a dangling medal or reward coin. Realistic materials and lighting, restrained reflection, soft diffuse contact shadow, no neon halo. The key feels tangible and original, like an exceptionally crafted product photograph rendered in 3D. Strong silhouette with clean negative space, no overlapping the copy. This is an illustrative personal contribution symbol, not a claim the app can operate a physical car lock.

Right half: RTL typographic composition. Small title "مفتاح الخير بإيدك"; beneath smaller "كل مساعدة بتفرق". Large navy "42" followed on next line by "مساعدة مكتملة". Below, minimal warm gold label "مساعد ذهبي". Smaller separate blue text "320 نقطة" at bottom of text group. Numbers are sample data for design review.

Bottom integrated strip across the card: generous white space and hairline divider, then text aligned right "باقي 18 مساعدة للمستوى الجاي". Under this a slim teal progress bar filled exactly70% from RIGHT to LEFT, grey remainder on LEFT. No extra labels, no imaginary functions, no floating icon collage, no stage strip under the card. Keep the artwork and copy balanced, compact and uncluttered. Typography clean and professional with Arabic dots fully separated, subtle rounded corners, precise spacing. AKHOO palette cobalt #1469e8, teal #0c9c9a, navy #102b44, pearl-white. Soft natural studio light, no special effects, no glitter, no watermark, no phone frame. High visual quality with true depth.
